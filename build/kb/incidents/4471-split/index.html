The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads day-first dates month first. Every tenant in the directory writes the day first. A booking for 7 April comes out as 4 July; a date with a day above 12 cannot flip, so only the first twelve days of each month are hit. All channels.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type MM/DD. Only through the form, only for tenants on booking-v3. People do what the hint says and type the month first; the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The dates flip like parser, but the fix is the form’s hint, not the parser. A parser fix alone leaves these reports open.

shift: the invite moves by one day. No month flips here. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May; a client in Lisbon got Tuesday while a client in Toronto got Monday for the same meeting; one invite arrived before the booking was made. That points at time zones or daylight saving, not at date order, and a parser fix will not touch it.
